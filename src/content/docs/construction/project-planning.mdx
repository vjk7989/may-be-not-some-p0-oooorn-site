Begin with one independently verifiable workflow. Record the expected outcome, authorized identities, required data, allowed tools, approval thresholds, evidence requirements, and safe failure behavior.

<Card title="A deployment plan must name" icon="seti:notebook">

- owners for data, model, policy, infrastructure, security, and business outcome;
- exact model endpoints, tools, resources, and destinations;
- credential scope, lifetime, rotation, and revocation;
- actions that are automatic, denied, or approval-bound;
- required evidence and its retention boundary; and
- rollback, cancellation, retry, and incident procedures.

</Card>

Use staged gates: map, implement, test, observe, then expand. Unknown dependencies block increased authority rather than becoming implicit defaults.
