Custom AI work begins where a workflow has a concrete requirement that the standard product boundary does not address directly.

<Card title="A bounded integration defines" icon="puzzle">

- the identity and tenant model;
- the smallest required data and tool surface;
- structured policy inputs and decision outcomes;
- credential issuance, expiry, and revocation;
- approval binding for consequential actions;
- evidence events and sensitive-data exclusions; and
- failure, retry, cancellation, and recovery behavior.

</Card>

Custom work does not imply unrestricted model access, a new general-purpose platform, or unsupported security guarantees. Prefer the smallest integration that satisfies the verified workflow.
