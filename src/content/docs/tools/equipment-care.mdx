import { Aside, Card, CardGrid } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Review policy">
    Revalidate permissions and limits after changes to models, tools, data
    sources, owners, or business impact.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rotate credentials">
    Expire and revoke grants that are unused, overbroad, or associated with a
    changed workflow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Monitor evidence">
    Alert on missing events, repeated denials, approval reuse, unusual
    destinations, and unexpected action volume.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Test recovery">
    Regularly exercise outage, retry, partial execution, cancellation, and
    evidence-restoration procedures.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Keep production, development, and evaluation identities separate. Define retention for operational evidence and restrict search, export, modification, and deletion by role and tenant.

<Aside type="tip">
  Reauthorize long-running agents periodically. A permission that was
  appropriate at deployment may become excessive after the workflow changes.
</Aside>
