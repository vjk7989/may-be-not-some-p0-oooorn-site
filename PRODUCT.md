# Product

## Purpose

Buckleson helps companies, organizations, and individual users apply explicit protection, execution control, credentials, and accountable evidence around AI workflows.

## Products

- **Hyper Tern:** identity, policy, routing, permissions, tools, resources, and action boundaries.
- **Hyper-ABS:** minimization, masking, redaction, tokenization, and abstraction before model inference.
- **Hyper-0x:** attributable execution evidence, verification, audit, and settlement. It is not a confidentiality mechanism or proof that a model output is true.
- **Hyper Wallet:** available agent credential wallet for identities, credentials, policy-bound permissions, and delegated approvals. It is not a digital-asset custody or payment product and does not guarantee secure outcomes.

## Services

AI Security, Secure Inference, and Custom AI engagements use qualified claims and explicit responsibility boundaries.

## YAGNI boundary

No authentication, payments, asset custody, database, CMS, analytics, account system, or production form backend. The site is a deterministic static export.
