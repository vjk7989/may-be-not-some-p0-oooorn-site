# Design

The site preserves the pinned ScrewFast template's responsive composition and interaction families while replacing its demonstration identity with Buckleson.

## Visual system

- Warm neutral canvases, dark surfaces, amber emphasis, rounded cards, and the existing Tailwind/Preline component geometry remain the foundation.
- Official Buckleson and Hyper-0x marks are rendered from byte-preserved local source assets without distortion.
- Four approved cinematic Buckleson images are reused across heroes, product cards, articles, insights, and documentation. No hardware photography, customer logos, or portrait testimonials are rendered.
- Typography, responsive breakpoints, tabs, FAQ disclosures, mega menu, theme control, modal behavior, Lenis scrolling, and GSAP detail-page motion remain template-native.

## Accessibility

Content remains readable without animation; reduced motion suppresses nonessential effects. Controls retain visible focus, correct accessible names, and keyboard operation. Decorative images use empty alternative text; product imagery uses concise Buckleson-specific descriptions.
