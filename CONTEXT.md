# Buckleson domain glossary

- **Execution boundary:** The point where identity, policy, permission, tool, resource, and action controls are evaluated before an AI action proceeds.
- **Hyper Tern:** Buckleson product for controlled AI execution.
- **Hyper-ABS:** Buckleson product for masking, redaction, tokenization, and abstraction before inference.
- **Hyper-0x:** Buckleson's evidence infrastructure for attributable, tamper-evident records, verification, audit, and settlement.
- **Hyper Wallet:** Available agent credential wallet for identity, credentials, policy-bound permissions, and delegated approvals. It is not a digital-asset custody or payment product.
- **Marketing surface:** Astro pages for home, products, services, blog, insights, contact, and the custom 404.
- **Docs:** English Starlight pages generated from `src/content/docs/`.
- **Demo form:** Client-side validation and confirmation UI with no network submission or account creation.
